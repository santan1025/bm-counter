Who may sign in with Google

An email here can open the app with Google, with no PIN. Leave it empty and that person signs in with their phone number and PIN as before. Adding an email is granting access — nobody can grant their own.

Supabase is already the shop's live copy — this is a second copy you own outright, in an account nobody else controls. It matters if the subscription lapses or the database is deleted by mistake.

A browser can only hold Google Drive permission for about an hour, and cannot renew it quietly. So backups run while you are here and looking at the screen. There is no automatic overnight backup — that needs a server, which this app does not have.
